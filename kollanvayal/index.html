Native Roots & Heritage

Kollanvayal (கொல்லன்வயல்)

Affectionately celebrated as "Chinna Italy" (Little Italy) — an agricultural village in Aranthangi Taluk, Pudukkottai District, Tamil Nadu.

📍 10°13′05″N 79°00′22″E 📮 PIN 614622 ☎ STD: 04371 🚗 Reg: TN-55Z

Kollanvayal is my native village, situated in the heart of rural Tamil Nadu within the Aranthangi Taluk of Pudukkottai District. Surrounded by open skies and fertile agricultural plains, it is a serene village defined by deep familial bonds, enduring spiritual traditions, and an extraordinary connection to the wider world.

"In Tamil, vayal means a green paddy field. Kollanvayal is where my roots began, teaching me values of resilience, simplicity, and community that stay with me everywhere I travel."

Why is Kollanvayal Known as "Chinna Italy"?

Throughout Tamil Nadu and neighboring regions, Kollanvayal has earned the fond moniker "Chinna Italy" (சின்ன இத்தாலி).

Beginning several decades ago, enterprising young people and families from Kollanvayal travelled overseas—particularly to Italy—to build careers, trade, and provide for their loved ones. Over the years, this created an affectionate bridge between Mediterranean culture and rural Tamil Nadu.

Returning villagers brought back modern architectural ideas, introducing Italian-styled porticos, arches, and colorful facades alongside traditional courtyard homes. It is common to hear stories of Italian cities exchanged alongside tales of local farming in evening village gatherings.

Spiritual Landmarks & Temples

Temples form the spiritual and cultural heart of Kollanvayal. They are not merely places of worship, but gathering spaces where generations celebrate annual festivals, music, and harvest rites:

  • Sri Muthumaari Amman Kovil (ஸ்ரீ முத்துமாரியம்மன் கோவில்): The revered guardian deity of the village. The annual festival brings families from across the state and abroad together in joy, rituals, and feast offerings.
  • Sri Kannudaiyar Rakkammal Kovil (ஸ்ரீ கண்ணுடையார் ராக்கம்மாள் கோவில்): An ancient shrine of protective grace, deeply cherished by generations of local lineage.
  • Sri Karpaga Vinayagar Kovil (ஸ்ரீ கற்பக விநாயகர் கோவில்): Welcoming every journey and beginning with auspicious blessings.

Life, Landscape & Farming

Beyond its international links, Kollanvayal remains grounded in farming traditions. The landscape shifts with the seasons—from the emerald shimmer of newly sown paddy fields to the golden harvest under warm southern skies. Mornings greet you with the calls of peacocks, temple bells, and the gentle rustling of palm trees along village lanes.
